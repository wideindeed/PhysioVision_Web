Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 17, 2026

At Physio-Vision ("we", "our", "us"), we are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use the PhysioVision desktop application and associated online services.

1. Data Collection

We collect the following types of information when you use PhysioVision:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, and profile details provided during registration or through Google OAuth sign-in.
  • Exercise & Session Data: Exercise performance metrics, repetition counts, range-of-motion measurements, pain level logs, and session timestamps.
  • Device Information: Operating system version, application version, and basic hardware identifiers for troubleshooting purposes.
  • Usage Analytics: Aggregated and anonymised usage patterns to help us improve the application experience.

PhysioVision uses your device's webcam for real-time pose estimation. Video frames are processed locally on your device and are never transmitted, stored, or recorded by our servers.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the PhysioVision application and services.
  • Track your rehabilitation progress and generate performance reports.
  • Power AI coaching features that provide personalised exercise feedback.
  • Communicate with you about updates, support, and service-related matters.
  • Improve and develop new features based on aggregated usage data.
  • Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, or security incidents.

3. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored using encrypted cloud infrastructure. We employ industry-standard security measures including:

  • Encryption in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest (AES-256).
  • Secure authentication via Firebase Authentication and Google OAuth 2.0.
  • Regular security audits and vulnerability assessments.
  • Access controls limiting data access to authorised personnel only.

While we strive to protect your personal information, no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

4. Third-Party Services

PhysioVision integrates with the following third-party services:

  • Google OAuth: Used for secure authentication and sign-in. Subject to Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Groq API: Powers the AI coaching feature. Exercise summary data (not video) may be sent to Groq's servers for generating personalised feedback. Subject to Groq's Privacy Policy.
  • Firebase: Used for authentication, database, and cloud storage services.

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

5. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing of your data.
  • Export your data in a portable format.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details below.

6. Contact

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us at:

Email: privacy@physiovision.app
