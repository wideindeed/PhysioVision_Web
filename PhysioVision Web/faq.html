Setup & Hardware

What equipment do I need?

Just a Windows PC with a webcam. PhysioVision uses your built-in or USB webcam for pose tracking — no wearables, depth sensors, or special hardware required.

What are the system requirements?

Windows 10 or Windows 11, 64-bit. A webcam capable of at least 720p. A reasonably modern CPU — the app uses MediaPipe for pose detection which runs efficiently on most hardware.

Does it work on Mac or Linux?

Currently PhysioVision is Windows-only. Mac and Linux support may come in the future, but there's no timeline yet.

How should I position my webcam?

Place your camera so your full body is visible in the frame. A distance of about 2–3 metres works best. The app will guide you with on-screen positioning cues.

Exercises

What exercises does PhysioVision support?

PhysioVision currently supports 10 clinical exercises:

  • Knee Extension
  • Wall Pushup
  • Hip March
  • Shoulder Extension
  • Shoulder Scaption
  • Deep Squat
  • Sit-to-Stand
  • Pushup
  • Bicep Curl
  • Lateral Raise
How does exercise scoring work?

Your form is analysed frame-by-frame using LSTM neural networks trained on the UI-PRMD (University of Idaho Physical Rehabilitation Movement Dataset). The model compares your joint angles against clinically correct form to produce a form score for each rep.

Can I add my own exercises?

Not currently. Each exercise requires a dedicated trained model. We may expand the exercise library in future updates.

AI & Technology

How does the AI coaching work?

After each rep, your form data is sent to Groq's LLaMA 3.1 8B Instant model, which generates personalised spoken feedback about your technique. The coaching is specific to each rep — not generic tips.

What pose tracking technology is used?

PhysioVision uses Google's MediaPipe Pose for real-time body landmark detection. MediaPipe extracts anatomical keypoints from your webcam feed, which are then processed by our scoring models.

What neural network architecture is used?

The app uses a mixed architecture built on Keras 2.15 / TensorFlow 2.15:

  • Bidirectional LSTM for UI-PRMD exercises like sit-to-stand and deep squat
  • Standard LSTM for exercises like bicep curl and lateral raise

All models were trained on the UI-PRMD (University of Idaho Physical Rehabilitation Movement Dataset).

Does the AI run locally or in the cloud?

Pose detection and exercise scoring run entirely on your local machine — no video is sent anywhere. Only the spoken coaching uses a cloud API (Groq) to generate feedback text, which is then spoken locally.

Achievements & Progress

What achievements can I earn?

There are 8 achievement badges, each with Bronze, Silver, and Gold tiers:

  • First Rep — Complete your first repetition
  • Consistent — Maintain a regular exercise schedule
  • Dedicated — Show long-term commitment
  • Centurion — Reach 100 total reps
  • Perfect Score — Achieve a perfect form score
  • High Scorer — Consistently score above thresholds
  • All-Rounder — Complete sessions across multiple exercises
  • Pain Warrior — Continue exercising while managing pain levels
How does streak tracking work?

PhysioVision tracks your daily exercise streak. Complete at least one session per day to maintain it. Your current and longest streaks are displayed on your dashboard.

Can I set goals?

Yes. The goals system lets you set targets for sessions, reps, and scores. Progress is tracked automatically and visible on your dashboard.

Privacy & Data

Is my webcam video recorded or uploaded?

No. Your webcam feed is processed locally in real time for pose detection. No video frames are ever recorded, stored, or transmitted to any server.

What data is synced to the cloud?

Only session metadata is synced: exercise name, date, form scores, rep counts, and pain levels. This data is encrypted before transmission and stored in encrypted cloud storage.

How secure is my account?

PhysioVision supports Google OAuth for secure authentication. If you use a username/password instead, your password is hashed server-side and never stored in plain text. All API communication uses HTTPS.

Account

How do I create an account?

You can sign up with Google OAuth (one click) or create a username/password account. Visit the sign up page to get started.

Can I sign in with Google?

Yes. Google OAuth is fully supported both on the website and in the desktop app. It's the fastest way to get started.

Is PhysioVision free?

PhysioVision is currently free to use. Download it from the download page and create your account.
