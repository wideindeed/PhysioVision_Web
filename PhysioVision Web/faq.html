Setup & Hardware

What equipment do I need to use Physio-Vision?

All you need is a standard Windows desktop or laptop and a built-in or external webcam. Our software handles all AI processing locally — no motion-capture suits, depth cameras, or special hardware required.

What webcam do you recommend?

Any webcam capable of 720p at 30fps or higher works well. Most modern laptop cameras meet this. External USB webcams like the Logitech C920 give noticeably better tracking in low light.

What are the minimum system requirements?

Windows 10 or 11, minimum 8 GB RAM, and an Intel i5 / AMD Ryzen 5 or better. A dedicated GPU is recommended for smooth 30fps inference but is not required.

Medical & Safety

Is this a replacement for a physical therapist?

No. Physio-Vision is an advanced supplementary tool for monitoring form during home exercises. It is not a medical device and does not replace professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult your physician before starting a new exercise regimen.

What if the AI gives me incorrect feedback?

While our models are trained on extensive clinical data, no automated system is perfect. Always use Physio-Vision as a supplement to professional oversight. If feedback seems off, pause and consult a licensed professional.

Privacy & Data

How is my data secured?

Your video feed is processed 100% locally on your computer — no video is ever uploaded. Only numeric joint-angle data and session scores are AES-256 encrypted and synced to our secure database.

Do you sell my data?

Never. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal or session data with any third party. Fully anonymised aggregate statistics may be used internally to improve model accuracy.

Platform

Can I use it on a Mac or mobile device?

Currently the app is optimized for Windows 10 and Windows 11 to leverage native GPU acceleration. Mac and mobile companion apps are on our roadmap — stay tuned.

Does it work offline?

Core AI analysis runs fully offline. An internet connection is only needed to sync session history to the cloud and to download model updates.

Availability

When will the app launch publicly?

We're in a closed Beta phase to validate neural network accuracy. Public launch announcements will go to our waitlist first. Sign up now to be among the first.

How do I join the Beta?

Create a free account and select your experience level. Beta slots are allocated per cohort. We'll notify you via email when your access is granted.
